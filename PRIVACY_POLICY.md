# Privacy Policy for Meditary

**Effective Date:** January 5, 2026  
**Last Updated:** January 5, 2026

## Overview

Meditary ("the App") is a meditation tracking and journaling application designed with **privacy as a core principle**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

### Local Data Only
Meditary operates entirely offline. All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is **never transmitted to external servers or cloud services**.

**Data stored locally includes:**
- Meditation session records (date, duration, type, gong sound preference)
- Meditation journal entries (customizable questions and your answers)
- User preferences (language, theme, reminder time)
- Device identifier (generated locally, never transmitted)

### Device Identifier
The App generates a stable device identifier and stores it exclusively on your device. This identifier:
- Is **stored locally only** and never transmitted to any server or third party
- Is **not used for analytics, tracking, or any remote purpose**
- Cannot identify you personally and cannot be linked to your identity, location, or behavior
- Is stamped on each meditation entry and session record to support a **planned future feature**: when you switch phones, you will be able to export your data and restore it on a new device without needing an account, using this identifier to confirm data ownership locally

## 2. Data You Control

You have complete control over all data in Meditary:

- **View:** Access all your meditation records and journal entries anytime
- **Edit:** Modify or delete any entry or question at any time
- **Delete:** Remove individual entries, questions, or all data from the app

There is no account, login, or authentication required. Your data belongs entirely to you.

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect

Meditary **does not collect, store, or transmit:**
- Your name, email, or contact information
- Your location or GPS data
- Your meditation content or journal entries (stored locally only)
- Your device usage patterns or behavior
- Cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics identifiers
- Any health or fitness data beyond meditation duration
- Any biometric data

## 4. Third-Party Services

Meditary does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App is completely self-contained and does not communicate with external servers.

**No data is shared with:**
- Advertising networks
- Analytics platforms
- Social media services
- Cloud storage providers
- Any other third parties

## 5. Security

Your data is protected by:
- **File system encryption:** Your data is protected by iOS Data Protection and Android's encrypted storage — provided by the operating system, not by the App
- **No network transmission:** Your data never leaves your device
- **No account compromise:** Without authentication, there is no account to compromise
- **Device security:** Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features

## 6. Children's Privacy

Meditary does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is designed for adults practicing meditation. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided information to the App, they should delete the App and all associated data.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Local data:** Stored indefinitely on your device until you delete it
- **Cloud backup:** On iOS, AsyncStorage data may be included in iCloud backups as part of standard device backup behavior. To prevent this, you can disable iCloud backup for Meditary in your device's Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage
- **Deletion:** You can delete any or all data at any time through the App's settings

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
